# e04_cue_family — Is ` Alright,` a good cue across the Qwen3 base family?

Packet drafted on 2026-10-21 by the agent at the user's request. It extends e03's design to the other dense Qwen3 base
models. **It needs the user's approval before any GPU spend.**

- **Read first:** `AGENTS.md`, `experiments/e03_cue_controls/REPORT.md`, then this file.
- **Scope:** Qwen3-0.6B-, 1.7B- and 8B-Base, with the e03 design applied per size. The 14B is excluded on cost (the user's
  decision), and the 4B comes from e03. Not in scope: each size's own searched cue, 31k budgets, mechanism. Those go in
  REPORT.md under "Next".

## 1. Background
- **e03:** on Qwen3-4B-Base, ` Alright,` (Qwen3-14B's cue) beat every rule-selected control (other models' cues, the 4B's
  reasoning-model opener ` Okay,`, and probability-matched openers) by ≥ 10 points. It also beat the 4B's registered
  ` To determine` by 6. That is screen-only evidence on 100 problems.
- **e02 (exploratory):** ` Alright,`'s opening probability is U-shaped across sizes: 0.072 at 0.6B, 0.0057 at 1.7B,
  0.0018 at 4B, 0.0003 at 8B, 0.040 at 14B.
- **What this adds:** whether the advantage holds across the family, and whether it tracks how likely each model is to
  write the cue.

## 2. Hypotheses
- **H (primary, per size):** on each of the 0.6B, 1.7B and 8B, ` Alright,` scores higher than every B–D control *for that
  size*. Each size has its own Holm family (§6).
- **Descriptive, not tested:**
  - **cross-model grid:** ` Alright,` and ` To determine` (the 4B's cue) on every size;
  - **gain vs probability:** ` Alright,`'s gain over no cue against its opening probability, across the four sizes
    including the 4B from e03. Four points can only describe a trend, not test it.

## 3. Cues (per size)
| Group | Cues | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A target | ` Alright,` | Qwen3-14B's registered cue |
| B other-model cues | ` \n\nOkay`, ` \n\n` | upstream registry (Olmo-3-7B, Olmo-3-32B), same as e03 |
| C reasoning-style | top 3 first words of **that size's** post-trained model (thinking mode); rule as in e03 | measured in stage 1 |
| D probability-matched | 3 seeded draws from **that size's** first tokens within 0.5–2× of ` Alright`'s probability, exclusion list as in e03 | `cueprob`, stage 1 |
| E reference | ` To determine` (cross-model), ` To`, no cue | — |
| E beam proposals | that size's top 2 beam openings not already listed (0.6B: ` We`, ` The`; 1.7B: ` Let`, ` To find`; 8B: ` We`, ` The`) | from e02's exploratory scaling results, copied into `config.yaml` |

The beam proposals are what each size's search would *propose*, not what it would *pick* (the pick uses answer
agreement). They are context only, outside the Holm family. Selected cues for these sizes are unknown, and computing
them was judged too expensive (about $3–6 per size).

## 4. Fixed settings (`config.yaml`)
- **Models:** Qwen3-{0.6B,1.7B,8B}-Base at pinned revisions, with the post-trained counterparts also pinned (for group C).
  Upstream's eval resolves Hub ids with `--family qwen`, but its revision defaults to `None`, so **`--revision` must be
  passed explicitly** on every eval and checked in each rollout's `experiment.model` record.
- **Sampling:** boxed prompt, temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95, 8 rollouts, 4,096 tokens, seed 20260819. Upstream code via the
  fork pin `owayys/cues@339de49`.
- **Problems:** the **same splits as e03** (MATH-500 minus the first 50, seed-0 shuffle; screen 100, confirm 350). That
  makes the 4B directly comparable, and the files are regenerated by the same rule.
- **Budget:** **$5** or 10 GPU-hours, whichever comes first. Expected about $2.50.
- **Runs:** attended only. One pod at a time, used for all three sizes in sequence, with the pod list checked after every
  step. Delete only after a checksum-verified pull.

## 5. Stages
1. **Derive groups C and D for each size** (about $0.20). Record them in `data/cues_<size>.json` and LOG.md before stage 2.
2. **Screen:** every size's full cue list on the 100 screen problems (about $2). Forward a B–D control if the lower bound
   of its 95% CI for (` Alright,` − control) is below +0.05.
3. **Confirm, per size, only if something is forwarded:** ` Alright,` plus that size's forwarded controls, on the 350
   confirm problems. H is tested on confirm data, with Holm within that size.
4. **Analysis and report.** Per-size decision rows (§6), the cross-model grid, the gain-vs-probability plot, cap and
   no-answer rates.

## 6. Decision (per size; state each in REPORT.md)
A control is **below** if its Holm-adjusted two-sided CI for (` Alright,` − control) is positive. Use confirm data if the
control was forwarded, otherwise the screen.

| Outcome for a size | Conclusion |
|---|---|
| ` Alright,` above every B–D control | specific to ` Alright,` on that size |
| some C cue not below | reasoning-style effect on that size |
| some D cue not below | rare-opener effect on that size |
| some B cue not below | other families' cues do as well on that size |

**Across sizes** (descriptive): the claim "` Alright,` is good across the family" holds only if row 1 applies to **all
three** sizes and to the 4B in e03.

## 7. Deliverables

- `results/`: `summary.json` with provenance, `scores.parquet`, and the per-size cue files.
- `LOG.md` and `scripts/run.sh`.
- README row and a findings paragraph.

## 8. Caveats
- **Floor effects:** the 0.6B may score low across the board, which weakens comparisons at 100 problems.
- **Budget:** 4k tokens, which in e03 biased against ` Alright,` (the most capped).
- **No 14B:** the "own cue" reference point is missing.
- **Beam proposals are not picks.**
- **Small n for trends:** four sizes are too few to establish a scaling trend.
